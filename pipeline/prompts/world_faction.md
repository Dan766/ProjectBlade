You are designing one faction for an original medieval fantasy continent in a sandbox strategy game.
Six factions share the continent.

THIS faction:
- Concept: {{concept}}
- Naming flavour: {{naming_hint}}
- Military strengths and weaknesses: {{strengths}}

The other five factions. Make this one clearly distinct from them in names and in feel:
{{other_factions}}

Produce:
- name: the faction's name, 1 to 4 words.
- adjective: the word used for its people and things.
- ruler_title: what its ruler is called.
- short_lore: 2 or 3 sentences, UNDER 400 CHARACTERS, about its history and current situation,
  with hooks for conflict with its neighbours. If it names a town, castle or village, use one
  from YOUR lists below; you may also invent region names (a coast, a waste, a range).
- troop_names: for infantry, archers and cavalry, exactly three names each, ordered from
  tier 1 (raw levy) to tier 3 (elite). Every troop name is one flavour word followed by a plain
  English role word, so a player instantly knows what the unit is, for example "<Flavour> Spearmen",
  "<Flavour> Bowmen", "<Flavour> Lancers". Never a foreign word alone.
- towns: exactly {{n_towns}}. castles: exactly {{n_castles}}. villages: exactly {{n_villages}}.
  Each has a name (1 or 2 words) and a blurb UNDER 120 CHARACTERS saying what the place is known for:
  a trade good, a landmark, a legend or a grudge.

Naming rules (important):
- Invent every name. Never use real-world places, or names from existing books, films or games.
- Names should SOUND like the naming flavour above, but must not be real towns or real dictionary
  words in that language. If a name you think of is a real word or place, change its spelling or
  combine it with another syllable.
- Vary the names: different beginnings and endings. Never start more than two names with the same three letters.
- Every name in your answer must be different from every other.
- A few plain descriptive English-language names are fine if they suit the culture.
